How to edit

  • Click a region to select it. Drag a filled handle to move a vertex.
  • Drag a hollow handle (edge midpoint) to add a vertex. Double-click a vertex to remove it.
  • Hold Shift and drag inside a selected region to move it whole.
  • Draw new region: click to place vertices, double-click or Enter to close, Esc to cancel.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan. [ ] change section, Delete removes the selected region, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes.
  • Draw each hemisphere as its own region. Overlaps are resolved in favour of the smaller region.

Export and import

Export saves vmh_boundaries.json. Put it in the repository at config/vmh_boundaries.json. Edits are also kept in this browser between visits.
